Research interests

I am an incoming graduate student at the MIT Media Lab with Pat Pataranutaporn under the Cyborg Psychology group. Broadly, my research interests lie in developing methods to study long-horizon agentic behavior with mechanistic and attributional interpretability. I hope to draw on these insights to develop novel human-computer interaction paradigms and affordances that amplify human capabilities.

Right now, I am working as an AI & ML Intern at Noblis developing in-house solutions for feature attribution (e.g., 1, 2, 3) and studying how selecting certain target outputs as objects affect attributional faithfulness and plausibility.
